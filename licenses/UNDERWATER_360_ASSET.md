## Native-detail regional cleanup, 2026-11-12

The new 4K and 6K images are edited derivatives of the same genuine 9500 × 4750 Heron Bommie SVII photograph, with the same attribution and **CC BY-SA 3.0 Unported** terms and modification-notice obligations above. Processing: verified original → Lanczos downsample to 6144 × 3072 master → ten local AI-assisted fish-removal regions (native 1254 × 1254 outputs, reduced to 1024 × 1024 regions) → harmonic boundary color matching and short edge feathering. This is an edited photograph, not an untouched photographic record. The failed 1774 × 887 cleanup is not enlarged or used as a source for these new files.
